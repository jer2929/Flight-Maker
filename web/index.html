My minimums & profile

Everything below lives in this browser and gates every proposed flight. It persists between sessions and adjusts as your flying evolves. Defaults are the built-in starting card.

Wind (kt) - maximum
Density altitude (ft) - advisory only

Never a NO-GO. When density altitude sits this far above aerodrome elevation you get an amber row telling you to check takeoff and climb performance - the call is yours. NAV CANADA broadcasts it at 200 ft; the default here is 500. Computed from an observed METAR for a departure now, and from the model forecast for a departure later - the row says which.

VFR ceiling (ft AGL) - minimum
VFR visibility (SM) - minimum

Your personal minimums can be higher than the published takeoff/approach minimums (e.g. require 2 SM even where a ½ SM takeoff is legal).

IFR ceiling (ft AGL) - minimum

A flat personal floor - set it at or above the published approach minimums (DA/MDA) you'll actually fly.

Night operations

Whether a night flight stacks a threat on the decision card. Some pilots treat night as their biggest risk multiplier; others, current and over familiar terrain in stable VMC, don't. Night still selects your night ceiling and visibility minimums either way.
